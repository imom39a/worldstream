WorldStream Negotiate

One deal. Four seats. One inspectable history.

Explore how a self-hosted WorldStream venue could coordinate independent buyer and seller agents, an exact-proposal human approval, a venue signer, restart-safe reconnect, privacy, replay, and evidence—without making the runtime the negotiator.

Prototype question: Does the responsibility boundary feel understandable when the whole first run is concrete—from approving one exact Pack bundle to exporting party and venue proof? The state machine below is pure, in-memory, and intentionally throwaway.

What this prototype does not claim
  • No server, durable database, real WebSocket, model invocation, wallet, or credential exchange exists here.
  • Fingerprints and signatures are readable simulations—not cryptographic verification.
  • Restart and reconnect demonstrate the intended state/cursor contract in memory; they do not prove crash recovery.
  • The A202 label is a compatibility design target, not an upstream conformance claim.
  • The UI is a question-answering shell. It is not a generated Pack UI or a production Studio design.

## Agent Heist prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original panoramic background artwork for a browser cooperative strategy game called Agent Heist. A moody near-future night-time heist, three covert operatives on a rain-slick rooftop overlooking a monumental high-security vault tower in a dense futuristic city. Stylized premium game key art, painterly 3D illustration with crisp silhouettes and rich cinematic environment, noir atmosphere. Composition: landscape 2:1, the three crew members grouped in the right third, one hooded route scout, one compact visor-wearing tech insider, one long-coated broker, seen mostly from behind. The vault tower and distant city fill the center, LEFT THIRD is dark atmospheric city and negative space suitable for readable interface text overlay. Deep ink and midnight blue shadows, cool cyan and teal edge lights, small warm amber lighting on the vault doorway, subtle rain and mist. Grounded science-fiction, restrained saturated highlights, elegant detailed environment. No text, no lettering, no logos, no UI, no borders, no charts. Make the environment beautiful and visible rather than crushed to black. This is an image asset, not a website screenshot.

## Negotiate prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: landscape game cover artwork, no UI. Original editorial game key art for a multi-party negotiation activity called Negotiate. A sophisticated dramatic negotiation chamber, an oval dark wood table under a sculptural warm brass pendant light, four empty leather chairs around it, carefully placed paper dossiers and a small brass balance-scale centerpiece. Panoramic city windows at dusk behind the table. Intimate strategic atmosphere, high quality painterly 3D premium board-game art. Color palette distinct from a blue cyberpunk game: rich burgundy and aubergine shadows, warm copper and gold lighting, deep walnut materials, small sunset highlights. Composition 2:1 wide, tabletop and chairs centered toward the right, upper left reserved as clean dim negative space for separately rendered title. It should suggest diplomacy, negotiation, and consequential agreement without implying combat or fantasy gameplay. No readable text, no logos, no watermarks, no UI, no people, no weapons. Keep detail and contrast visible in a small game-library card.

## WorldStream crossroads prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original panoramic environment background for WorldStream, a game library where people and agents choose among distinct activity packs. This is environment artwork only, NOT a UI mockup. Create a lavish hand-painted cinematic game key art panorama, wide 2.4:1 composition. Scene: an otherworldly crossroads at blue hour, weathered dark stone terraces and a luminous stream winding between monumental doorways. On the RIGHT HALF, two elegant enormous doorframes open onto distinctly different environments: one contains a rain-soaked futuristic city lit in muted cyan and amber; another reveals a rich warmly lit walnut council chamber with brass and burgundy. These are evocative vistas, not hard rectangular image collages. A small traveler in a simple coat and a small round companion automaton stand on the lower-right terrace, seen from behind, looking into the possibilities. The LEFT 45 PERCENT is quiet atmospheric charcoal-teal mist and subtle distant cliffs with low detail and low contrast, intentionally reserved for large legible HTML headline and buttons. Warm amber horizon light, moody deep forest/teal shadows, painterly texture, rich atmospheric perspective, beautiful crafted game environment with wonder and invitation, no harsh neon, no purple. Architectural details and the two illuminated gateways are concentrated right of center and remain readable in a wide short crop. No letters, logos, titles, icons, UI controls, frames, watermarks, grids, weapons, HUDs, technical diagrams, or fake interface elements. Do not imitate any existing game's specific characters or architecture.
